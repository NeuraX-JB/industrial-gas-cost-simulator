Sources and calculations

How the replay works

Henry Hub cash is the EIA’s monthly average spot price in U.S. dollars per million British thermal units (MMBtu). Replay covers complete November–March winters only. The plant profiles are illustrative consumption schedules; editing a month creates a custom profile. EIA monthly Henry Hub series.

For each month, the mixed budget is consumption × [fixed share × fixed price + floating share × Henry Hub cash + local basis + variable delivery] + the monthly service charge. The all-variable case sets the fixed share to zero. Local basis may be negative.

The calculation assumes the contracted share matches actual monthly consumption and settles against the same monthly cash index. It excludes taxes, supplier premiums, financing, margin, volume deviations and timing differences. Monthly averages can differ from an actual plant’s daily load-weighted price.
