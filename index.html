A $17,231.35 difference in the 2025–26 replay

The illustrative mill uses 46,900 MMBtu. Buying at monthly Henry Hub cash prices costs $238,559.00 including delivery and local basis. Fixing half of each month against the reconstructed September 30, 2025 curve brings the bill to $221,327.65.

January alone accounts for $17,537.85 in savings. Fixing adds to the bill in February and March. Open the simulator and select Reset scenario to inspect all five months or change the plant’s load.

Which prices could a buyer have seen?

The floating bill uses EIA monthly averages. Dated fixed prices come from fund positions disclosed in later SEC filings. Only three completed winters have an eligible curve. You can enter an assumed flat quote for any of the 29 winters.

Both plans pay the same local charges. Daily load variation, taxes, supplier premiums, financing, margin and contract volume mismatches can change a plant’s actual bill.
